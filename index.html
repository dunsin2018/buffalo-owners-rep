01  —  THE MODEL

How the model works

In the United States, client contracts are executed through our United States operating partner, which carries the insurance for United States operations. Behind those contracts, the project management and owner's representative services are provided as a contracted service.

02 — Services

What we do

Six defined services. Taken together they cover a project from first scope meeting to final closeout document.

02.1

Scope & Budget

Walk the property, define exactly what is — and is not — in the project, and build a realistic budget before anyone is asked to price it. This is where most overruns are prevented.

→
02.2

Scheduling — Primavera P6

A proper Primavera P6 schedule with milestones and a visible critical path, updated as work proceeds — so a delay is seen weeks ahead, not discovered on the completion date.

→
02.3

Tendering & Bid Review

Prepare bid packages, issue to suitable contractors, level the bids on a like-for-like basis, and give you a clear recommendation. You make the award.

→
02.4

Coordination

Coordinate your contractors day to day: site reviews, progress meetings, requests for information, and sequencing — so trades are not waiting on each other or on decisions.

→
02.5

Change Order Review

Every change is checked for scope, price, and schedule impact before you approve it. No surprise extras approved on site without your sign-off.

→
02.6

Reporting & Closeout

Regular written progress and budget reports in plain language, then a managed closeout: punch list, warranties, manuals, and final documentation handed over complete.

→
03 — Ways to Engage

Matched to how you own

Package A

Renovation & Rehabilitation Management

For: Out-of-Town & Canadian Owners

For owners who bought in Buffalo but cannot be on site — including Canadian and diaspora investors. You get a trusted manager on the ground side of the project, reporting back in writing.

  • Rehab scope & budget before you commit
  • Tendering to licensed local contractors
  • Site reviews with photo progress reports
  • Change control & closeout pack
Package B

Capital Project Owner's Representation

For: Apartment, Commercial & Church Owners

For a defined capital job — a roof, parking lot, building envelope, unit turnovers, or a larger planned project — where the cost of getting it wrong is high.

  • Capital scope, budget & P6 schedule
  • Bid levelling & award recommendation
  • Coordination through construction
  • Payment review & formal closeout
Package C

Portfolio Facilities Retainer

For: Self-Storage & Multi-Property Owners

Ongoing monthly coordination across a portfolio — the recurring repairs, upgrades, and small capital jobs that otherwise fall on the owner or site staff.

  • Rolling list of works, prioritised with you
  • Contractor coordination across sites
  • Access, security & systems upgrades
  • Monthly written portfolio report
